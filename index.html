Follow the logic

Biochemistry reasoning practice for chapters 1 to 12 of Lehninger Principles of Biochemistry, 8th edition

Memorizing facts does not build understanding. Understanding comes from following the logic: what does this need to be, what do I already know, and where does it fit. Each question here is broken into the steps a biochemist would take. Write your thinking at every step before you check it. After each chain you will connect the idea to everyday life and to medicine.
